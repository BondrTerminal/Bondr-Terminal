Recommendation

Start from Preview 1: Institutional Terminal, then selectively borrow the stronger BONDR brand treatment from Preview 2 and the spacing discipline from Preview 3. For this product stage, credibility beats flash. The rebrand should say: “serious execution terminal with visible safety,” not “generic crypto dashboard.” Preview 1 gives the best foundation for professional trust, while still letting vivid tangerine become the signature BONDR edge.
